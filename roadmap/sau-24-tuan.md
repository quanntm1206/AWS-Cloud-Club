# After 24 weeks: choose your next direction

You don't need to decide on your career from the first week. After the capstone, look back at what made you want to dig
deeper. The three directions below all use the same foundation as the roadmap; differs in the skills you prioritize in 90
the next day.

## Direction 1 - Model Engineering

Suitable if you like data, experiments and error analysis. Choose a new tabular problem; build strong baselines,
ablation design, calibration and subgroup analysis. Target after 90 days: a reproducible experiment report,
not a long leaderboard.

## Direction 2 - ML Platform / MLOps

Suitable if you like packaging, testing, CI and operations. Expand local capstone with data validation and model registry
small, batch inference, monitoring schema/drift and rollback drill. AWS is just an option; you can simulate the whole
pipeline on personal machine.

## Direction 3 - Applied Computer Vision

Suitable if you like photos and transfer learning. Choose a small dataset with a clear license, keep frozen-backbone baseline,
Try exactly one fine-tuning change, then analyze errors by class and image condition. Use Colab Free or Kaggle
Free with accelerator; Always keep CPU-mini fallback.

## A realistic 30-60-90 day calendar

- **30 days:** choose a direction, re-read the relevant competency milestones, re-run the capstone from a clean environment.
- **60 days:** make a hypothetical change; Add testing, failure analysis and reproduction notes.
- **90 days:** 5-7 minute demo, stating trade-offs and unresolved issues; No need to keep the cloud endpoint running.

If you don't know which direction to choose, start with Model Engineering. It forces you to understand the data and evaluation before
add infrastructure or larger model.
